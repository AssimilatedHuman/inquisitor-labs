The Problem: AI Has Replaced Trusted Discovery

Customers still use search engines like Google, and overall search volumes remain stable. But this activity now plays a limited role in decision‑making. People perform an initial search, then turn to chatbots when they need advice, comparisons, or guidance.

Customers already ask AI about your brand -whether it’s safe, legitimate, worth the money or better than competitors. AI answers with whatever narratives it has absorbed. This audit shows you exactly what AI is telling your customers when they ask.

Where they once asked friends, checked reviews, or browsed multiple sites, they now ask conversational AI systems. These systems simplify brands, omit important details, misclassify categories and confidently recommend competitors. Your brand can lose visibility even while search traffic looks stable -because the real decision‑making layer has moved into AI.

Scale of the shift

  • 1 billion monthly active users on ChatGPT.
  • 900 million weekly active users.
  • 198.8 million daily visits.
  • 29,000 prompts processed per second.
  • 557 million mobile app active users (Aug 2025).
  • chatgpt.com is the 5th most‑visited site globally with 5.4 billion monthly visits.
  • Questioning is the largest interaction category (18.3%), showing users treat ChatGPT as a decision‑making interface.

The Consequences: Distorted Market Position

When AI systems misrepresent your brand, the customer journey breaks:

  • Your brand goes missing from relevant categories
  • Incorrect or outdated descriptions are given as accurate
  • Your brand is replaced in favor of competitors
  • Brand presence weakens in high‑value paths
  • Recommendation frequency reduces
  • Confident but inaccurate comparisons are made

Most people don’t realise that AI chatbots routinely present incorrect “facts” with a tone of authority, creating an impression of expertise. As a result, customers can be steered into choices that are not in their best interests. These distortions divert demand and revenue directly to competing brands -whilst your brand loses prestige and money, one confidently wrong AI conversation at a time.
